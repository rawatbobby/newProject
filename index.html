OUR WAY OF MAKING

How Our Pahadi Sweets Are Made

A little patience, good ingredients and plenty of care go into every mithai we make.

  1. 01

    Pure Pahadi Khoya

    We slowly simmer pure milk from the hills into rich, creamy khoya for our traditional sweets.

  2. 02

    Clean at Every Step

    We take care to keep our ingredients, preparation area and packing clean and hygienic.

  3. 03

    Made Fresh with Care

    Each batch is prepared with attention to its flavour, texture and the little details.
